# Kanto Gear 3.0.0 — Silph Link OS

Kanto Gear 3.0 debuts **Silph Link OS**, a new high-resolution companion system
inspired by the Nintendo DS generation. This is not a reskin of the old tab
bar: apps, widgets, a customizable Home screen and consistent touch-first
flows rebuild how the companion screen is organized and used.

## Highlights

- A customizable, touch-first Silph Link Home screen with as many persistent
  pages as needed, app icons, live widgets and long-press editing.
- Silph Store for installing or removing optional Kanto Gear apps and their
  widgets, with App of the Day, recommendations, previews and `NEW` promotion.
  It changes local UI preferences only and never downloads code.
- Dedicated Explorer, Map, Party, Pokédex, Bag, Trainer Card, Step Counter,
  Field Kit, Store and Options apps, plus a coming-soon Notes preview.
- App icons and widgets are separate Home surfaces. Packages can provide both,
  and Field Kit supports multiple one-tap widgets for unlocked tools.
- Explorer combines the live route map, contextual encounters, trainers,
  visible items, hidden items and Itemfinder interaction without turning the
  screen into a table of data.
- Redesigned Party, battle, move, item-target, move-learning, message and PC
  flows with consistent touch feedback, focus styling and transitions.
- Four-segment battery display, day-period icons, status icons, type badges,
  HP/EXP information and fainted-party treatment designed for the larger HGSS
  canvas.
- HGSS Auto follows Gold, Silver and Crystal's night period and applies the
  same 18:00 boundary to the device clock in Red, Blue and Yellow.
- Gen 1 and Gen 2 field items, TMs/HMs and PP items continue through the
  original game actions while their selection screens are presented below.
- Silph Link Options groups appearance, display, battle, research and control
  settings, with immediate persistent changes, explicit Home and option resets
  and context-sensitive rows.

## Compatibility

- Pokémon Red, Blue and Yellow.
- Pokémon Gold, Silver and Crystal.
- Fullscreen Swap, Combined Screen and Separate Screens.
- Touch, keyboard and controller input; the HGSS Home editor intentionally
  remains touch-only.
- German, Spanish (Spain) and French companion language packs are included as
  separate optional ZIPs.

## Upgrade notes

- The first 3.0 launch switches every installation to **HGSS Light**. Selecting
  HGSS Dark, HGSS Auto or a Legacy theme afterwards is saved normally.
- Kanto, version-matched, Modern, OG, SGB and Advanced themes remain available;
  3.0 does not delete them.
- The first HGSS launch creates the default Home layout. Later edits, installed
  apps and widget choices are stored immediately and survive restarts.
- Newly promoted optional apps are not installed automatically. Open Silph
  Store and choose **GET** if you want them.
- Silph Store installs are UI preferences, not network downloads. Every app
  already ships inside Kanto Gear.
- Kanto Gear contains no ROM, ROM-derived asset or save data.
