Privacy & connection

Connect shares Gear's visible screen (including names and Notes) with this phone and sends your touches and controller buttons to the game. ROM and save files are not transferred.

Everything is served by your game device over local HTTP/WebSocket. No accounts, analytics, cookies, access logs, cloud relay or third-party page requests. Live network addresses and inputs stay in memory; this feature does not record the screen.

Only use a trusted private network: HTTP does not encrypt or protect traffic from someone monitoring or modifying your Wi-Fi. Do not expose port 8765 to the internet. The QR key and code expire after five minutes and work once. Disconnect in Settings, close this page, or change Gear's display mode to stop sharing.

Preferences stay in memory unless you enable “Remember settings”. You can remove saved preferences with “Forget saved settings”. Pairing keys are never saved in browser storage. Your browser or QR scanner may keep its own history.
