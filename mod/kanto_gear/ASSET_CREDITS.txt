Kanto Gear asset notes
======================

Bag icon
--------
The front-facing green-and-amber daypack is an original Kanto Gear design,
drawn by the icon renderer in ui/skins/icons.lua. It is covered
by Kanto Gear's MIT license. No external bag sprite is loaded or bundled.

Fonts
-----
The three bundled PNG files are generated font atlases, not game sprites.
Their sources and regeneration tool are in tools/hgss_font_atlas in the
source repository. See ui/fonts/FONT_CREDITS.txt, ui/fonts/HGSS_PIXEL_FONT_LICENSE.txt and
fonts/ for the applicable third-party font notices and licenses.
